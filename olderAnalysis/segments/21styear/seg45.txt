She chugs one last sip, then takes off… She's surprised Alexander opens his door. She can see right behind him, his bathroom. The door still all frosted glass. Now fixed. She refuses to enter, hands him two zip-locks. "Margaret's nail-clippings and hair. They're good for DNA too… "You're morbid." He goes to close the door in her face. Sasha starts stripping.
