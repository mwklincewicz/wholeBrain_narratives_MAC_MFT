Clara takes an electric candle out of a Halloween lantern. They won't allow open flames at the hospital. She ducks as she enters Sasha's hospital room. The hand-made Happy Birthday Banner still hits her on the back. She places the electric candle on the cupcake. Sasha blows and Steven turns off the flame. The nurses applaud. Clara keeps hearing the sound of the baby's heartbeat in her head. Faster than a bird's, faster than she could ever have imagined, faster than fear. This sound, she thinks, is the opposite of death.
