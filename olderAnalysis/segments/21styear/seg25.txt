When people hear someone has died they cover their mouths, as if to protect their own breath. Gary does that. "Oh my God!" And that's how she knows. "It's Jeannie and Tommy's baby," he says. "They found him. In his crib. He's gone." Gary reaches and holds her. Clara leans on his chest. Gary holds Clara's hand and will not let it go. "I'm so glad you're here," he whispers. Their palms are sweaty. That's how they find comfort. Together.
