
I think you know what happened next. Pie Man emerged from behind the late-night library drop-box, made his delivery, and fled away, crying, “Ego sum non an bestia.” Or at least that’s what it said in my story in the newspaper the next day, which ran with a photo of him leaving the scene, cape flowing behind him, doing this. And that’s what made him a sensation on campus. People started dressing like him and quoting him in class. The Ram ran five major stories about Pie Man, all of them by me.
