And we were wondering, are you Pie Man?” And I knew by the way she said it, I knew that if I said yes, she would have sex with me. And wasn’t I really Pie Man? For having brought him into being. Didn’t she only know about him through me? But she had asked me a straightforward question that came with a straightforward answer. In fact, I wasn’t Pie Man. As far as I knew, I had never seen the guy out of costume. So I looked at her and I said, “Yes, Angela. I am Pie Man.” And she said, “Oh, good. Now buy me a beer and tell me all about it.”
