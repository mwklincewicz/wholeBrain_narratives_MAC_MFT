So, I whip out my notebook and I march right up to him, I say, “Dean McGowan, is it true that Fordham plans to raise tuition substantially above the inflation rate and, if so, wouldn’t that be a betrayal of its mission?” And he looks at me like, “What are you talking about, punk?”
