"How could you do this to me?" Clara hisses at Gary. "When did you find out about New York? When did you even apply?" Anger and humiliation are drowning her breath. And probably a touch too much wine. But Gary is giddy and thrilled and sees nothing wrong with their lives. "Baby -- be proud," he laughs. "It's just for a year, we'll work it out..." And what is so sad -- he really believes this…
