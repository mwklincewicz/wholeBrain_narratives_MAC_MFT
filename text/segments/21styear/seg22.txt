Clara puts the birthday card in an envelope and walks out, into the night. What if she kept walking and just ran away? The mailbox door creaks and the cool air feels good… Back at the house, Gary's upstairs. Left the lights on, their bottles of wine; his and hers. His is done, hers almost. Clara returns to the kitchen, mindless tidying... Until she finds their joint AMEX card. Messy torn envelope. Among messy papers. The charges are for photographic equipment. And new luggage and a ticket, only one ticket, one way to New York, expensive clothes, and a fancy hotel in Tribeca. It's the price tag that gets her… He sits up in bed all defenses. "You've got to spend money to make money." "But you're not making any money" "I'll pay for it. Like you said, I'll ask my parents." "What a mess you've made…" "Stop talking. Just stop talking Clara!" She stares at the number. Thirty-six-thousand dollars in the last two months. She feels she can't breathe. This is half her salary
