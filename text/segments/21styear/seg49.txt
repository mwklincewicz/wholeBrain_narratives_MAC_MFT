Sasha walks into Alexander's offices. She's calm. Rubs her eyes. She's suddenly cold. She wants to sleep. All she wants is to sleep. She's already dreaming, Margaret shows her a window: "This is the way out," she says. Sasha wants Alexander to see her. She wants him to watch. Across the room: Clara notices this girl dozing over, a volunteer falling asleep on the job. So much stress. But then she sees Alexander walking over to her. Shaking the girl. Yelling at her. Suddenly angry, unrecognizably angry, then scared. Young people, congregate, pull rashly away…
