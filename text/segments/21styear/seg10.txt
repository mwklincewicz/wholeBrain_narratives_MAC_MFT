"I don't want to go back to New York," Clara says quietly. The dishes from last night are still in the sink. Gary has a meeting with his advisors to discuss the internship. Of course he's late. And now the dishes won't be done until this evening... Crusted chili. Soap, bubbles. Scrub! She's shopped and cooked and served and now dishes, again. "I'll do them later, Clara, don't fuss," Gary says as he flips over tupperware, newspapers -- looking. "You've seen my wallet?" "How long is this internship Gary?" " September to June." "Without getting paid? I can't afford to float this whole place by myself." Gary heads to the door: "I'm sure you can be a journalist in New York, too" But she just signed her contract here and he knows it. "We should have talked this move through, before you applied..." He laughs. "You would have talked me out of it." For a moment he looks like a boy, getting caught and standing up in defiance. Almost charming. But he is thirty-five. "What's good for me Clara, is also good for us." That certainly used to be true. Is it now?
