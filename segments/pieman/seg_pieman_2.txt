
And one day I’m walking toward the campus center and out comes the elusive Dean McGowan, architect of a policy to replace Fordham’s traditionally working- to middle-class students with wealthier, more prestigious ones. So I whip out my notebook and I go up to him and I say, “Dean McGowan, is it true that Fordham University plans to raise tuition substantially above the inflation rate, and if so, wouldn’t that be a betrayal of its mission?” And he stops and looks at me and he says, “Listen up, punk.”
