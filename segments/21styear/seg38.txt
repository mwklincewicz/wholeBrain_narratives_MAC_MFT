In the distance, Gary's mother is fighting with a janitor, "I got to go!" Clara blurts, and she runs. "This bulletin board is just for the church," the janitor yells. "Enough is enough, I clean up these fliers every day… These people are dead…" Clara finds herself screaming: "It's her son…have a heart," and then, she suddenly sees it: A missing flier, discarded, on the ground. It's Margaret's face, xeroxed a few too many times. Any information, please call. Clara picks up the flier… She looks at Margaret: black dress, and white pearls and her elegance. "Mother and wife" says the flier. "Please, Call."
