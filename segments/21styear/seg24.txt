Thirty-six-thousand dollars. Clara is scared, she hates Gary and in a flash she wishes him dead. She walks to the living room. She should sleep on the couch. Once, in New York, when Gary lost their money in the stock market he slept in the closet. She turns on a light. Terrified of being alone. Defeated, tormented, pacing, then hating herself more than him, she crawls back to their room and their bed. Desperate to recapture his smell. She spoons him, her face in his neck. "Gary, Please!" And then his cell rings...
