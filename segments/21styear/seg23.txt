Alexander's third martini arrives with dessert. He hands Sasha the olives. She shakes her head, no. He acts disappointed. "I thought you like risks..." She likes this man. He's fun. "So when do I start?" "It's funny, how you look just like Margaret," he suddenly says. Sasha licks the chocolate off her spoon. "We all look alike: My mother, me, Margaret..." Alexander waves for the check. "You look like her but you're nothing like her. You're not a girl who likes playing safe. Of course that can get you in trouble…" A shadow crosses her eyes. "Did my dad tell you why I took a year off? I got addicted -- to prescription pills." She holds onto the candle between them. "I'm glad you told me," says Alexander. "I don't believe in lies." "You tell me a truth, then." Her voice takes Alexander by surprise. "I'd like to show you my apartment," he says. Sasha reaches for his martini glass. "I'll be twenty-one next month and no one is looking."
