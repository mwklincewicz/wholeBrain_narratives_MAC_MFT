And this really raises the question of what we inherit. Can you have a trait that you never get to use because you don’t encounter an environment that triggers it? In other words, are the Bronx guys like Mutarelli tough because they live in the Bronx? Or are they tough because they inherit some sort of toughness—inherent toughness—from their tough Bronx parents that is then triggered by their tough Bronx neighborhoods? And conversely, why am I a wimp? Is it nature? Is it nurture?
